# DSA AI Coach Curriculum

The platform contains a massive, dynamic knowledge graph of DSA concepts. Problems are highly curated and fully executable via our backend test-harness.

## Personalized Paths

The system dynamically reprioritizes modules depending on the selected career track:

*   **Backend Engineering**: Prioritizes Hashing, Trees, Graphs, and Concurrency.
*   **Frontend Engineering**: Prioritizes Arrays, Strings, DOM Trees, and State Management basics.
*   **Machine Learning**: Prioritizes Matrices, Math/Bit Manipulation, DP, and Advanced Graphs.
*   **Cybersecurity**: Prioritizes Bit Manipulation, Strings/Parsing, and Graph Traversal.
